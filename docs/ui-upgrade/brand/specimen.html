Grid
32 units. The staff lines are 2 units thick on even units (8, 14, 20), so at 16 px each lands on a whole pixel.
Field
Corner radius 7 of 32. Content sits inside the 5-unit margin (dashed).
Staff
Ink at 42% strength. Never full strength: the sweep is the one solid thing.
Sweep
Leaves the lowest line at 1.6 units and swells to 4.6 at the top: a crescendo, not a tick or a swoosh.
Ahead
Staff lines 16, 13 and 8 units long; a hairline edge at 30% so the mint field holds on paper.
Minimum size
16 px for the mark, 120 px wide for the full lockup.

Lockups: lg, md, sm
